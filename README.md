# Pipex

**Software development company based in Bolivia 🇧🇴**

We design and build software solutions that help organizations improve their processes, automate operations, and turn ideas into reliable digital products.

## What we do

* **Custom software** — Business systems and specialized solutions tailored to each organization's requirements.
* **Web applications** — Modern, responsive, and scalable applications for business and operational needs.
* **Mobile applications** — Cross-platform and native mobile experiences for Android and iOS.
* **Backend & APIs** — Reliable services, integrations, and APIs that connect systems and automate workflows.
* **Infrastructure** — Deployment, server administration, CI/CD, and the infrastructure required to operate our applications.

## Our approach

We focus on solving real-world problems with practical technology.

Our projects combine:

* Clean and maintainable software
* Modern development practices
* Automation and integration
* Reliable infrastructure
* User-focused interfaces
* Long-term maintainability
